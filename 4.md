Mathematical Model & Core Mechanics
The game uses specific mathematical models across its three main scaling mechanics:
 * Compound Multiplier Damage Scaling (Hyper-Carry Phase):
   
   
   Note: Because Skill Effect and Boss Damage operate in their own separate scalar brackets, stacking them yields higher returns than adding more saturated flat Attack.
 * Fortress HP Multiplier Pivot (Stage 50+ Transition):
   
   
   When enemy burst exceeds frontline unit defense ceilings, swapping from single-target ATK to multi-line HP% engines coupled with Scorpii allows the Fortress hull to absorb frontline incoming damage.
 * Constellation Stardust Optimization:
   The Stardust economy features zero-penalty reallocation. The simulator below lets you test and contrast:
   * Early Game (1–30): Thorn Blade (ATK) + Stepping Stone (Onboard ATK)
   * Mid Game (30–50): Lyre (Support) + Jibril/Valentine burst scaling
   * Late Game (50+): Scorpii Iron Gate (HP Multiplier) + Leonis Sharpshooter (Attack Speed hitstun lock)

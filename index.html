Comprehensive Meta Progression Guide

Phase 1: Early Aggression (Stages 1–30)

Prioritize direct ATK scaling with gold and constellations (Libra Thorn Blade). Focus on soft-capping Crit Rate at 50%.

Phase 2: Summoner Stall & Multipliers (Stages 30–50)

Switch to Violet for wave blocking and Zelos for invulnerability frames. Saturate carries with Valentine (+200% ATK).

Phase 3: The Fortress HP Pivot (Stage 50+)

Unlock Scorpii Iron Gate Nebula, equip multi-line HP% Legend Engines, unlock Engine Slot 2 at Augmentation 5, and run Styria (+300% HP).
